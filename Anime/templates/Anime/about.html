Anime Moment is a small Django site about anime. The home page lists titles with their season, and the New section holds articles written by the site staff.

Who writes the articles?

Staff accounts. They sign in through /admin/login/ and then add, edit or delete articles right on the site. Visitors read everything without an account.

What is on the Creators page?

Short notes about the studios and people behind the shows. An admin fills them in through the Django admin.

Where do the pictures come from?

Each card stores a link to an image. The artwork belongs to the studios and rights holders of each anime.

TERMS

Anime Moment is a non-commercial study project. Anime titles and artwork belong to their rights holders. The site runs as is, without guarantees.
